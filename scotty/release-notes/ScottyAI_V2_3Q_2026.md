## Customer changelog

- Smart alternator support: with Feature In switched on, Scotty keeps charging from the low side to the high side so a smart alternator does not go to sleep.
- Various reliability improvements.

## Compatibility

- Applies to all Scotty AI Version 2 models - the firmware version on your Scotty's home screen should start with 2 (2.0 up to 2.6.3.2).
- Only for units set to a 250 kbit/s CAN bus speed. Some Version 2 units run at 500 kbit/s, or have custom firmware - do not update these; contact Safiery Support.
- Do not install on Scotty AI V3 or V4 - different hardware; the wrong firmware can permanently damage the unit.
- After updating, check your settings on the Tune and Advanced pages are still correct before using the unit.
- If you are unsure which Scotty you have, contact Safiery Support before updating.
